🍫 How to make a brownie

1. Drag flour, milk, chocolate and butter from storage into the bowl.

2. Hold your mouse on the bowl until the batter is mixed.

3. Drag the batter pan into the oven.

4. Wait for the brownies to finish baking.

5. Drag the brownies onto the cooling rack.
They will stay there while they cool.

6. Buy a box from the shop.

7. Drag the cooled brownies into the packaging area.

8. Hold the package to seal it.

9. Click the shop counter to sell it.
