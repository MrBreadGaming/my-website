📜 Grandma Sheepie's Brownies
INGREDIENTS NEEDED:
🌾 1x Wheat Flour
🧂 1x Sugar Sack
🍫 1x Chocolate Slab
🥛 1x Milk Jug
BAKING STEPS:
  1. Drag each of the 4 ingredients from the shelf into the mixing bowl.
  2. Click and swirl your mouse inside the bowl to stir the batter to 100%.

  4. Place hot brownies on the cooling rack and click to package them.
