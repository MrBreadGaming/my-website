📜 SECRET BROWNIE RECIPE

Classic Brownie

  1. Drag 1 flour into the bowl.
  2. Drag 1 sugar into the bowl.
  3. Drag 1 chocolate into the bowl.
  4. Drag 1 milk into the bowl.
  5. Hold the HOLD TO MIX button until the batter is ready.
  6. Drag the finished batter into the baking pan.
  7. Drag the filled pan into the oven.
  8. Press BAKE.
  9. Wait for the brownie to finish baking.
  10. Drag the baked brownie onto the cooling rack.
  11. Wait until the brownie has cooled.
  12. Buy a brownie box from the shop.
  13. Drag the cooled brownie into the box.
  14. Press PACKAGE.
  15. Drag the packaged brownie onto the counter.
  16. Press the $ button to sell it.
