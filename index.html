📜 SECRET BROWNIE RECIPE

Classic Brownie

  1. Drag 1 flour into the bowl.
  2. Drag 1 sugar into the bowl.
  3. Drag 1 chocolate into the bowl.
  4. Drag 1 milk into the bowl.
  5. Hold the MIX button until the batter is ready.
  6. Drag the batter into the baking pan.
  7. Put the pan into the oven.
  8. Press BAKE.
  9. Wait for the brownie to finish baking.
  10. Put the finished brownie onto the cooling rack.
  11. Buy a packaging box from the shop.
  12. Put the cooled brownie into the packaging box.
  13. Press PACKAGE.
  14. Put the packaged brownie on the counter.
  15. Press the cash register to sell it.
