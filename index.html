📖 Grandma's Fudgy Brownie Guide

Follow these steps carefully for maximum cozy deliciousness!

1
Gather Ingredients: Drag Flour, Sugar, Chocolate, and Milk from the Pantry shelf directly into the Mixing Bowl.
2
Whisk Batter: Hold the WHISK BATTER button until the mixing progress reaches 100%.
3
Fill Baking Pan: Drag the mixed bowl into the pan on the counter to pour the creamy brownie batter.
4
Bake to Perfection: Drag the filled pan into the Oven door and press START OVEN.
5
Cool & Box: Move hot brownies to the Cooling Bench, then drag cooled brownies into the Bakery Box.
6
Serve Customers: Hand boxed brownies over to waiting customers at the counter to earn Coins and Stars!
