🍫 How to play

Welcome to your tiny brownie bakery.

Start by dragging ingredients from your storage into the mixing bowl.

Once you have enough ingredients, hold your mouse on the bowl to mix.

Later you will be able to drag the batter into a pan, bake it, cool it, package it, and sell it in your shop.

The bakery is designed to grow slowly. Take your time. There is no rush.
